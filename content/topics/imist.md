---

- **I · Identification**: Age, sex. Your name and callsign.
- **M · Mechanism / Medical Complaint**: Mechanism, or presenting complaint.
- **I · Injuries / Information**: Injuries found, or related clinical info.
- **S · Signs**: RR, HR, BP, SpO2, GCS, Temp.
- **T · Treatment / Trends**: Treatment given, and the trend.
- **A · Allergies**: Drug, food, environmental.
- **M · Medications**: Current meds, recent doses.
- **B · Background**: Past medical history.
- **O · Other Information**: Social factors, valuables, family contact.
