title: Glasgow Coma Scale
slug: gcs
summary: Score consciousness from eye, verbal, and motor response.
aliases:
  - GCS
  - consciousness score
---

Sum for a total out of 15 (minimum 3).

## Eye Opening (max 4)

- **4** · Spontaneous
- **3** · To Voice
- **2** · To Pain
- **1** · None

## Verbal Response (max 5)

- **5** · Oriented
- **4** · Confused
- **3** · Inappropriate Words: No conversation.
- **2** · Incomprehensible Sounds: Moaning, no words.
- **1** · None

## Motor Response (max 6)

- **6** · Obeys Commands
- **5** · Localizes Pain
- **4** · Withdraws from Pain
- **3** · Abnormal Flexion: Decorticate posturing.
- **2** · Abnormal Extension: Decerebrate posturing.
- **1** · None
