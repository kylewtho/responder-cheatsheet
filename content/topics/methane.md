- **M · Major Incident**: Declared, or standby.
- **E · Exact Location**: Location and best access point.
- **T · Type of Incident**: Nature of incident, e.g. MVA, collapse, fire.
- **H · Hazards** ⚠: Present and potential hazards.
- **A · Access / Egress**: Best routes in and out.
- **N · Number of Casualties**: Approx. number and severity.
- **E · Emergency Services**: Present, and any additional required.
