- **1 · Walking?**: Can walk → Priority 3 (Delayed).
- **2 · Breathing?** ⚠: Open airway. Still not breathing → Deceased.
- **3 · Respiratory Rate**: RR <10 or >29/min → Priority 1 (Immediate).
- **4 · Perfusion**: Cap refill >2s or no radial pulse → Priority 1 (Immediate).
- **5 · Otherwise**: Cap refill ≤2s, pulse present → Priority 2 (Urgent).
