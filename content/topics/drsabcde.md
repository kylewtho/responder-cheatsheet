- **D · Danger**: Yourself, bystanders, patient.
- **R · Response**: AVPU · Alert, Voice, Pain, Unresponsive.
- **S · Send for Help / Severe Haem.**: Blood sweep, TQ, pack wounds.
- **A · Airway**: Open, clear, maintain. Consider NPA.
- **B · Breathing**: Rub and rake, chest seal, rate.
- **C · Circulation**: Abdomen, fractures, wounds, pulse.
- **D · Disability**: AVPU, pupils, ears.
- **E · Environment**: Space blanket, IMIST handover.
